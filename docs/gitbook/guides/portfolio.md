# Portfolio

[Portfolio](https://turret.capital/portfolio) brings your pooled Borrow and Earn positions, token P2P loans, NFT loans and withdrawal balances into one view. Connect your wallet on Robinhood Chain to load its positions.

Use **All positions**, **Borrowing** or **Lending** to focus the list. **P2P history** shows settled loans and cancelled or expired offers.

## Pooled Borrow and Earn

Borrow positions show the collateral deposited and the debt including interest. Select **Manage loan** to open that market and manage your collateral or repayment.

Earn positions show your estimated lending value and the amount **Withdrawable now**. These can differ because some of the pool's USDG is lent out. Select **Manage lending** to deposit or withdraw in the relevant market.

See [Borrow](borrow.md) and [Earn](earn.md) for the steps and rules for each product.

## P2P loans and offers

P2P entries show the market, loan ID, principal, collateral and current status. Active loans include the full repayment amount, due date and final deadline. Select **Manage P2P loan** to open the offer or loan and take the available action.

An open lending offer has reserved funding. An active loan has committed funding. Neither amount is available for immediate withdrawal. See [P2P loans](p2p.md) for repayment, cancellation and default settlement.

## NFT loans and offers

**NFT loans & offers** lists the collection, token ID and loan status. Open **Manage NFT loan** to review acceptance, repayment, cancellation, default or withdrawals. USDG and NFT withdrawals are separate actions on that loan page.

Use **Load more NFT loans** to fetch older positions. Totals cover only loaded positions and do not assign a market value to NFTs. See [P2P NFT loans](nft-loans.md) for the full workflow.

## Ready to withdraw

Settled P2P funds stay in their market until you withdraw them. Portfolio lists both USDG and collateral credits under **Ready to withdraw**. Select **Withdraw funds** to open the relevant market and confirm the withdrawal.

The overview total for P2P USDG does not include collateral tokens. Each token balance is shown separately below. Current token loans distinguish recorded credit from the amount available in that loan’s vault. Review any shortfall before withdrawing or permanently writing off an unpaid credit. NFT recovery is shown with its individual NFT loan.

## Keep your view current

Use **Refresh positions** after a transaction. If a market cannot load, retry it; a loading error does not mean the position has closed. Totals are marked as partial while data is missing or refreshing. Use **Load older loans** when reviewing earlier P2P activity.

If a P2P loan is missing from the list, open **Find a loan by its ID** on P2P and enter the market and loan ID from its link.

Portfolio groups your positions for convenience. Each position keeps its own collateral, funding and settlement rules.
