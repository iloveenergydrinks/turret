# Welcome to Turret

Turret is a lending platform on Robinhood Chain. You can borrow USDG against supported tokens or NFTs, supply USDG to lending pools, or agree to a fixed-term loan with another user.

Collateral secures each loan. As a borrower, you keep exposure to the token while it is locked in the lending contract. Repaying lets you recover your collateral; liquidation or default can cause you to lose it.

## What you can do

| Product | How you use it |
