# Standing offers UX

Operate surface. Extend P2P stocks and memes inside the existing Borrow hierarchy. Audience: lenders supplying their own USDG and borrowers pledging supported tokens. Preserve the site's warm white surfaces, charcoal text, Caslon headings, real token logos and compact navigation. No new top-level Borrow tab, synthetic branding or emitted design notes.

THESIS: Compare usable lending terms and accept a loan, or create a reusable lender balance with a separate funding/signing review.
OWN-WORLD: Existing Turret forms, searchable collateral picker and logo mapping; plain divided offer rows, restrained buttons, readable numerical terms.
STORY: Borrowers see the loan range, term, collateral and full repayment before signing. Lenders create their own contract, deposit, then sign offers. Both parties can find their loans again by wallet and recover funds through the existing facility actions.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Clear standing-offer heading and local Find a loan/Lend USDG/My loans navigation. Borrowing starts with a collateral filter and lender terms. Setup review replaces the editable fields rather than repeating them.
FORM: Extension of the approved incumbent app, no replacement visual world. Funding, quoted capacity and active debt are different quantities. No illustration is needed for this task.

Limits and proof: offers share backed USDG and exposure; availability is fresh and cannot be added across duplicate offers. Full fixed interest applies even on early repayment. Repayment deadline plus 24h grace, then collateral claim; no price liquidation or oracle. Idle USDG is withdrawable, active principal is not. Account/history failures remain explicit, and incomplete scans never imply no loans. Wallet confirmation uses the current provider at click time. Reviews reset on wallet changes. Local fixtures are labeled test-only.

Open release work: complete real-token fork verification, security/release review and exact production package verification. Current local browser evidence is not mainnet deployment evidence.
