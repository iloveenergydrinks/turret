# Proposed $DOCK lender rewards

Updated September 5, 2026. The user has selected a developer purchase of 3% of the token's initial total supply, all dedicated to lender incentives across every Dockyard lending market. The purchase will be performed by the user. No token launch, purchase, rewards contract, or distribution has been executed in this task. The duration and market weights below remain proposed defaults.

Dockyard can pay lenders two separate returns: USDG interest generated by borrowers, and a temporary $DOCK incentive funded by the treasury. The incentive can run while utilization is zero. Its purpose is to make liquidity available while Dockyard builds borrower demand; increasing deposits alone will not create sustainable interest revenue.

September 6 update: the user selected USDG subsidies plus DOCK distribution for lenders, to increase USDG available for loans. The [lender bootstrap pilot](dockyard-bootstrap-pilot.md) adds a separate funded USDG reward stream to the share-staking design and proposes a smaller initial DOCK tranche while borrowing capacity is limited. Its dual-token accounting and independent claims extend the DOCK-only specification below. Exact budgets and dates remain unset. The 3% reserve remains entirely for lenders; the six-round schedule below has not been activated.

## Obtain the rewards inventory

The standard PONSv2 token mints its entire supply to the bonding curve. The creator has no minting privilege or free allocation. Dockyard must buy $DOCK or receive contributed tokens before committing them to rewards. Confirmed in the [PONSv2 token source](https://github.com/ponsdotdev/ponsfamily/blob/main/contractsV2/src/v2/PonsV2LauncherToken.sol).

Creator trading fees accrue in the pairing asset. They can fund later purchases, but future trading volume is not a funded rewards budget. PONSv2's optional built-in buybacks release tokens over five years; they cannot supply an immediately available launch campaign. See the [official PONSv2 documentation](https://docs.ponsfamily.com/v2).

The rewards allocation target is `floor(initialSupply × 300 / 10000)` token base units. Record initial supply at launch so subsequent burns cannot redefine the committed token quantity. The entire acquired 3% is the lender reserve; there is no additional team slice within it. Activate rewards only against tokens actually received. Any purchase shortfall must be resolved before claiming the full 3% has been funded.

Proposed funding flow:

1. Quote the user's developer purchase targeting 3% of initial supply. The percentage is a token-output target, not an unlimited spending authorization: the actual pairing-asset cost, output protection, and launch configuration still need verification before the user signs.
2. Receive the acquired $DOCK in the rewards treasury multisig. Publish initial supply, the target and received quantities, and the funding transaction.
3. Allocate the funded reserve across all eligible markets and transfer each scheduled campaign's inventory to its corresponding rewards contract before activation. Keep future campaign inventory reserved for lenders.
4. Direct creator fees to the treasury. Review any later fee-funded purchases separately; start a new campaign only after its tokens have arrived.

Resolve the current factory and configuration from official sources at launch time, then verify the deployed contracts and transaction simulation. Do not reuse a cached address, fee quote, or disabled launch preset.

## Cover all markets from the start

Every deployed Dockyard market open to lender participation is in scope. The production homepage inspected September 5 currently lists nine stock markets: AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, AMZN, META, NVDA, AMD, MU, and TSLA. The code also contains ORCL and the deferred CASHCAT/PONS markets; catalogue entries alone do not establish that a lender pool is live. Build the campaign manifest from verified deployed pools and their admission status at activation, not from this dated list. A normal stock-market session closure does not make an otherwise active lender pool ineligible.

Proposed defaults, subject to the funded reserve and an agreed schedule:

| Parameter | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Eligible markets | Every verified Dockyard pool open to lenders |
| Total lender reserve | The user's developer purchase targeting 3% of initial token supply |
| Distribution horizon | Proposed: 180 days, in six funded 30-day rounds |
| Budget per round | One sixth of the original reserve, with integer remainder assigned explicitly |
| Initial market weights | Proposed: equal budget per eligible market; review before later rounds |
| Reward inventory | Each market contract holds its own round's allocated $DOCK before starting |
| Distribution | Continuous, proportional to each wallet's staked pool shares |
| Utilization requirement | None during the pilot; idle liquidity is eligible |
| Claiming | Lender-initiated, available as rewards accrue |
| Rewards lock | None for the pilot |
| Unstaking | Return pool shares at any time, independently of claiming rewards |
| Treasury seed liquidity | Treasury leaves its seed shares unstaked |
| Later rounds | Public schedule and manifest before funding; no automatic extension or increase of the total reserve |

The 180-day horizon is a recommendation, not a user-selected term. It prevents the full 3% from being exhausted in the first month while preserving a 30-day review cadence across all markets. Review borrowing, external deposits, retained liquidity, and incentives per supplied USDG before setting the next round's weights. Equal starting budgets deliberately give smaller markets a stronger deposit incentive; a nearly empty market can concentrate its reward budget in very few lenders, so do not mistake equal budgets for equal lender returns.

For `R` funded reserve tokens, `N` eligible markets, and six equal rounds:

```text
round budget = R / 6
market round budget = round budget / N
market emission per day = market round budget / 30
```

For the current nine-market example, each market would receive one ninth of the total reserve over the full program if membership and weights stay unchanged. That is approximately 0.3333% of initial supply per market over 180 days. These are proposed allocation fractions, not a token price or a guaranteed yield. Use base-unit integer arithmetic and assign all rounding dust without exceeding the reserve.

Freeze each round's market list, weights, budgets, and dates before activation. Newly opened markets participate in the next round; existing earned rewards never change. Markets still commissioning do not receive an active emissions schedule. Failed funding or activation must leave that market's budget reserved instead of silently enlarging other markets' allocations. No-staker emissions remain unallocated lender-reserve inventory and do not justify increasing the next round above its planned tranche. Any reuse of those leftovers is separately published within the same overall 3% cap.

There is no wallet signup bonus, per-wallet multiplier, or reward for transaction count. Splitting the same shares across wallets must not increase total entitlement. Continuous accounting prevents a deposit and withdrawal in the same transaction from collecting historical rewards. It does not prevent capital from arriving solely for incentives, or eliminate leveraged farming; measure those effects before expanding.

## Contract integration

Use one `DockyardLenderRewards` contract per eligible pool, funded from the shared 3% reserve. Each contract's immutable staking token is that market's verified pool share token; all contracts use the same verified $DOCK reward token. The existing pools inherit ERC-4626/ERC-20 and have no rewards checkpoint integration today. Separate contracts allow rewards without replacing pools or modifying borrower accounting, and keep staked shares and claim obligations isolated by market.

Do not add raw share balances across different markets: their share prices can differ. Allocate token budgets between markets first, then distribute each market's budget by its own staked-share balances. The funding manifest must contain chain ID, token, engine, pool, rewards contract, campaign dates, and exact token budget for every market, reject duplicates, and reconcile the sum to the funded tranche.

The lender flow is:

```text
Deposit USDG in any eligible Dockyard pool
  -> receive that market's lender shares
  -> approve and stake those shares in that market's rewards contract
  -> accrue $DOCK while the shares retain their pool interest and loss exposure
  -> claim $DOCK, or unstake shares
  -> redeem returned shares through the existing pool when withdrawals are available
```

Unstaking returns shares, not USDG. Pool cash, market health checks, share value, and realized losses still govern USDG withdrawals. A rewards claim failure or exhausted reward balance must not prevent share unstaking. There is no dependency on an oracle to return the staking token.

Use the established reward-per-share accumulator approach: checkpoint elapsed emissions and the user's entitlement before every stake, unstake, and claim. For an interval with a constant total stake:

```text
interval reward = funded tokens per second × elapsed eligible seconds
user reward = interval reward × user staked shares / total staked shares
```

Split intervals whenever stake changes. Clamp accrual to the campaign's start and finish. When nobody is staked, advance the timestamp without assigning rewards; those tokens remain unallocated and cannot be captured retroactively by the next depositor. Preserve rounding remainder and account for token and share decimals using integer arithmetic.

Required behavior for implementation:

- `stake(shares)`, `unstake(shares)`, `claim()`, and `earned(account)` with events and explicit credited share balances. No transferable rewards receipt in the pilot.
- Transfer and accounting checks must preserve one actual pool share for every credited staked share. Reject incompatible fee-on-transfer behavior and use safe token transfers with reentrancy protection.
- A campaign starts only after actual reward receipt. Its budget, rate, start, and finish are public. A running rate cannot be arbitrarily rewritten or an end date silently extended.
- Track accrued unpaid obligations separately from future emissions. New campaign funding or recovery of unallocated tokens must never reuse tokens owed to existing claimants.
- Multisig administration may stop future emissions after checkpointing, including after a pool loss or retirement. Accrued claims and unstaking remain available. The administrator cannot confiscate staked shares or earned $DOCK.
- A later campaign must not reset existing claims. Dust and zero-stake leftovers remain explicitly accounted for.

The [Synthetix staking-rewards proposal](https://github.com/Synthetixio/SIPs/blob/master/content/sips/sip-68.md) provides background on this family of contracts. It is a design reference, not evidence that a new Dockyard implementation is audited or compatible without testing.

## Frontend behavior

Keep the current borrower-funded lender APR separate from token rewards. Before there is a configured and funded campaign, do not display a reward rate or claim that deposits are earning $DOCK.

For each live market campaign, show its emission in DOCK/day, the wallet's estimated DOCK/day, claimable DOCK, staked shares, and end date. The Earn index should identify reward availability on every market. Portfolio may sum claimable DOCK across markets because it is the same reward token, while retaining per-market claims and principal exposures. Explain that the estimate changes when other lenders stake or unstake. USDG position totals must include both wallet-held and staked pool shares without counting the same assets twice.

Actions are `Stake lender shares`, `Unstake lender shares`, and `Claim DOCK`, with the existing transaction review and recovery behavior. A share approval is not a completed stake. A stake is not a new USDG deposit. Unstaking must precede the existing pool withdrawal review; do not quote a larger immediate USDG withdrawal merely because shares can be unstaked.

Show emissions in token units until there is a suitable price source and meaningful market liquidity. If a dollar-denominated rewards APR is added later, disclose the price source, timestamp, remaining campaign duration, and eligible staked asset denominator. A thin launch-pool spot price must not turn a fixed token budget into a promised dollar return. Do not combine USDG interest and $DOCK emissions into a guaranteed APY.

## Verification before activation

Contract tests must cover staggered entry, partial unstaking, repeated claims, campaign boundaries, no-staker intervals, extreme amounts, rounding, and preservation of existing claims across funding changes. Invariants must establish share backing and that rewards paid plus outstanding obligations never exceed funded inventory. Include malicious-token and reentrancy cases. Multi-market tests must reject duplicate pools, mismatched staking tokens, wrong chain IDs, excess aggregate allocations, and counting the same reserve twice. Test different share prices, a market joining a later round, and failure of one market's funding or claims without corrupting other markets' accounting.

Integration tests must use the actual pool share implementation: interest accrual while staked, recognized losses, paused or retired markets, unavailable USDG cash, returning shares without claiming, and withdrawal through the existing stock proof checks. Confirm that a reward-token failure cannot trap the pool shares.

Verify the PONS-launched token, funding transaction, contract code, administrator, and campaign schedule before enabling the frontend. Rehearse deposit, stake, claim, unstake, and pool withdrawal on a fork. Independent contract review remains outstanding until performed.
