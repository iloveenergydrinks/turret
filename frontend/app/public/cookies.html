Cookies and browser storage

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Turret uses cookies and browser storage to remember consent and support features you request. Cookiebot manages your cookie choices. Optional categories are off until you choose to allow them.

What the categories mean

Necessary
Storage needed to provide the site or a feature you request, including remembering your cookie choice, maintaining a requested wallet connection and recovering pending transactions. Necessary storage does not require optional-cookie consent.
Preferences
Optional settings that remember choices beyond those needed to provide a requested feature.
Statistics
Optional measurement of how the website is used.
Marketing
Optional advertising or tracking across websites. Choosing this category does not itself enable an advertising service.

Storage used by lending features

Wallet software can store connection information on your device. Turret uses local storage for pending transaction hashes and campaign enrollment recovery, and session storage for temporary market data and compatibility with existing transaction recovery. These records help avoid losing track of a transaction if you close or reload the page. They are separate from optional analytics.

Session storage generally lasts for the browser tab's session. Local storage can remain until the feature clears it or you clear the site's data. Pending transaction records are cleared when reconciled. Clearing browser data does not cancel a blockchain transaction, repay a loan or revoke a token approval.

Change your choice

Use Cookie settings in the footer or the buttons above to review or withdraw consent. You can continue using necessary features after rejecting optional cookies. You can also remove cookies and local storage in your browser settings. This may disconnect your wallet session or remove local recovery information; your on-chain positions remain.
