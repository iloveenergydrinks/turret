Privacy policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This policy explains how Turret handles personal data when you use our website, lending interface and related support services.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Juan Ramón Mendoza Hernández, based in Spain. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, contact support@turret.capital.

Information we process

When you visit, our hosting and infrastructure providers process technical information such as your IP address, browser, requested pages, timestamps and error information to deliver and protect the service.

When you connect a wallet or use lending features, we process your public wallet address, network, balances, transaction hashes, loan and collateral details, and signatures you choose to provide. We do not need your private key or recovery phrase. Never send either to support.

If you create a profile, publish a loan request, negotiate terms, enroll in a campaign or subscribe to alerts, we process the information needed for that feature. This can include your avatar, wallet-linked profile, proposals, messages, campaign eligibility records and contact details. Support correspondence includes whatever information you send us.

Why we use it

We process wallet connections, signed requests, profiles, negotiations and requested notifications to provide the services you ask for and take steps at your request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We use necessary technical and security information for our legitimate interests in running a reliable service, preventing abuse and investigating failures (Article 6(1)(f)). We also process information where needed to meet applicable legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

Where consent is required for optional cookies or similar technologies, we rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it through Cookie settings. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Public activity and other recipients

Blockchain addresses, transactions, loan terms and collateral recorded on a public blockchain are visible to others. Public marketplace requests and wallet profiles are also visible to other visitors. Counterparties receive the proposals and messages you send them. Do not include information you want to keep private in public fields.

We use service providers for hosting, infrastructure, communications and consent management. These include Railway for hosting and Cookiebot by Usercentrics for cookie choices and consent records. Wallet and RPC providers process information needed to connect and read or submit blockchain activity. A wallet provider may have its own privacy policy and act as an independent controller.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to lawful requests or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

International processing

Some providers and public blockchain participants operate outside Spain and the European Economic Area. Public blockchain information can be accessed worldwide. Where a service-provider transfer requires GDPR safeguards, the applicable arrangement must use an adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for information about the safeguards applicable to your data.

How long information is kept

We keep off-chain information for the period needed to provide the relevant feature, resolve disputes, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations. The period depends on whether a request, profile, loan, campaign, support case or legal obligation remains active. Signature challenges and sessions expire; their validity depends on the feature. Pending transaction records in your browser are retained for transaction recovery until reconciled or cleared.

You can ask us to delete personal data we control. Some records may need to remain for legal or security reasons. We cannot remove confirmed records from a public blockchain or erase copies independently held by other people.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your request, such as proof that you control the relevant wallet; we will never ask for its private key.

Contact support@turret.capital. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or your local supervisory authority.

Automated contract execution

Smart contracts automatically apply the terms of the transaction you authorize, including repayments, liquidations or collateral transfers where applicable. Wallet and market checks help the interface show available actions. These public financial records can be linked to your wallet. Read the applicable loan terms before signing.
